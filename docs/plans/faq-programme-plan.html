FAQ Programme — Bazar Real Estate

Sprint plan · 20 November 2026 · Draft for approval · bazarrealestate.ae (EN + AR)

Put the right questions on every public page — the ones Abu Dhabi buyers, investors and tenants actually type into Google or ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude — with answers written so an AI engine can lift the first sentence and cite us. Same method as Indus Hydraulics: research → payload → checks → database write. After one code batch, content costs no deploys.

2The standard every FAQ must pass

  1. Search-shaped. Reads like a real query and names the thing: the area, project, developer, law or fee. “Can foreigners buy freehold on Saadiyat Island?” — not “Who can buy here?”
  2. Answer first. The first sentence answers on its own (≈25–40 words) so an AI engine can quote it; 2–4 sentences, 250–500 characters in all.
  3. Sourced. Every figure comes from our data (price, size, handover, payment plan, permit) or a named authority — ADREC, DARI, ADGM, ICP, Central Bank, the developer. No invented yields, prices or growth.
  1. Dated where it moves. Fees, visa thresholds, rents and prices carry “as of November 2026”, and get a re-check date.
  2. Specific to the page. No answer reused verbatim across more than a handful of siblings.
  3. Leads somewhere. 2–3 internal links per page: listings, the project, the area guide, the mortgage tool, a guide, the enquiry form.
  4. Bilingual and compliant. Arabic twin as a gated machine first draft (ADR-0008). No guaranteed returns, no “best/No.1” claims, permit numbers per ADREC advertising rules.

3Research: where the questions come from

  • Google: People Also Ask, autocomplete and related searches for each area, project and developer name, EN and AR.
  • AI engines: ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude the obvious buyer questions; note whom they cite — the pages to out-answer.
  • Competitors: Bayut, Property Finder, developer sites and Abu Dhabi brokers' area guides: what they answer, what they miss.
  • Forums: Reddit r/abudhabi and r/dubai, expat forums.
  • Our own leads: enquiry messages, concierge chat logs and on-site search terms (anonymised).
  • Search Console: ask the client for access (or an export): queries we show for but don't win.

Output per page type: a research brief: real queries, facts needed, sources, common confusions (freehold zones, DARI vs. DLD, resale before handover, golden visa thresholds).

4Built to be cited by AI engines

  • FAQPage JSON-LD on every page that shows FAQs — including the eight landings missing it today. No FAQ rich results since 2023; the value is long-tail matching and clean Q&A for AI engines.
  • Answer-first prose rendered in the server HTML (already true), with a visible “Updated” date on the FAQ band.
  • Crawler access: confirm robots.txt and Vercel let GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended in; add an llms.txt pointing at guides and area pages.
  • Entity consistency: RealEstateAgent / Organization schema with ADREC licence and sameAs links; same facts on every page. IndexNow (Bing, feeds Copilot) for every URL we touch.

6Build — one code batch, then data only

Code (one PR, one deploy)

  • FAQ section (EN + AR, up to 12) for guides, service sub-pages, developer profiles and tools, reusing the existing master-page faqList.
  • FAQPage JSON-LD on all marketing landings.
  • Links in answers: [text](/path), same-origin only, stripped to plain text in the JSON-LD.
  • Optional per-listing FAQ override, so a listing can carry its own questions.
  • llms.txt, robots check, IndexNow ping on save.

Content pipeline (no deploy)

  1. Research brief per page type, then one payload.json per wave: page key → FAQs, EN + AR.
  2. Automated checks: every figure traceable to our data or a cited source; banned-claims grep; near-duplicate check across siblings; length bounds; every link resolves to a live slug; Arabic present.
  3. run.ts writes each page in its own transaction to pages / developments.meta, revalidates, pings IndexNow. Off-peak, never during a deploy.
